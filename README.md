Political Comms provides a direct-to-carrier political texting platform that enables compliant, large‑scale SMS, MMS, and RCS messaging for campaigns, PACs, advocacy groups, fundraisers, and elected officials across the United States. Founded in 2019, the service offers fast delivery, high throughput, and tools for voter outreach, fundraising, and volunteer coordination.

**Areas:** Company · Political · Messaging · SMS · RCS · Campaigns
